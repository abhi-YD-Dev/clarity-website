Common issues

Check that Clarity has permission under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera / Microphone. If you denied a permission previously, you'll need to enable it there — the app can't re-prompt automatically.

On-device AI grading requires a supported device and OS version. If yours doesn't support it, Clarity automatically switches to a fallback grading method so the app still works — this is expected behavior, not a bug.

Go to Settings inside the app and choose the option to erase all data. This permanently removes every concept, topic, and test attempt stored on your device.
