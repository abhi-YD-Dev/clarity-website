Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE] — effective for Clarity version 1.0

Clarity is developed by Abhinav Yadav. This policy explains what information Clarity accesses, how it's used, and — just as importantly — what we don't do with it.

The short version

Clarity does not have a server. There is no account to create, and no user data is transmitted to us or to any third party. Everything you create in the app — concepts, topics, test attempts, and progress history — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage framework (SwiftData). We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or any third-party tracking libraries.

Permissions Clarity may request

  • Camera — used only when you choose to photograph a written answer or scan a document for import. Photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — used only when you choose to answer a question by voice. Speech-to-text transcription happens on-device; audio is not sent to a server for processing.
  • Photo Library — used only when you choose to import an existing photo (e.g. of notes) via the system photo picker. Clarity never scans your photo library in the background.

Clarity only requests a permission at the moment you use a feature that needs it, and each is optional — you can use the rest of the app without granting it.

On-device AI

Answer grading and question generation are performed using on-device intelligence where available on your device and OS version. No answer text, transcript, or photo is sent to Apple, to us, or to any other party as part of this process.

Data storage

All content you create — concepts, topics, questions, attempts, and calibration history — is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Clarity includes an in-app option to erase all of your data at any time from Settings.

Children's privacy

Clarity is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app has no server and no account system, no personal information is collected from any user, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised, and material changes will be described here.
